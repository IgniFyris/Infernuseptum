# 🔥Infernuseptum v1.0.0

A short 2D platformer created for the <b><a href="https://campfire.hackclub.com/quezon-city"> 2026 Campfire Kyusi</a></b> 
and <b><a href="https://itch.io/devlogs"> Ember</a></b> Game Jams.
<p> Explore the realm beneath the mortal surface as a lost soul, overcoming unique mechanics and challenges to finally ascend... Or meet your final fate. </p>

<b> Playable on Browser: </b> https://ignifyris.itch.io/infernuseptum-demo
